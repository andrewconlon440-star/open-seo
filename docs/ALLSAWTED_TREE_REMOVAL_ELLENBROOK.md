## Tree removal planned for your property

Removing a tree can involve more than cutting the trunk. Access for workers and equipment, surrounding structures, fences, gardens and the remaining stump all need to be considered before work begins.

Allsawted assesses each job individually and recommends a practical approach for the site. Depending on the property and the condition of the tree, the work may involve removal, selective pruning, cutting timber into manageable sections, stump grinding or clearing the surrounding area.

## When tree removal may be needed

You may need a tree assessed when it is:

- damaged by storms or showing unstable growth;
- dead, declining or creating concern near a building;
- interfering with planned construction, a shed, paving or landscaping;
- blocking access or taking up space needed for another use;
- dropping limbs or creating an ongoing maintenance problem; or
- unsuitable for its current position.

If you are unsure whether removal is the right option, explain what is concerning you and include clear photos when requesting a quote.

## Tree pruning and storm-damage work

Complete removal is not the only possible outcome. Pruning or shaping may be more suitable when the aim is to manage growth, remove damaged material or improve clearance around the property.

Allsawted also assists with storm-damage prevention and making affected areas safe. Urgent hazards should be kept clear until the site can be properly assessed.
