Signal House approach

One production partner from the first idea to the finished edits. We plan the message, capture the footage and turn it into content your business can keep using.

A good shoot creates more than one good video.

A business film should make something easier for your customer. It can explain a service, show the quality of your work, introduce the people behind the business or give a campaign the energy it needs. We begin with that purpose, then plan the story and the shoot around it.

We produce video for Perth businesses across websites, advertising and social channels using professional production equipment, including DJI drones for aerial footage where it suits the story and location. We look for useful moments that can become a main film, shorter edits, vertical clips, still images and future posts. Production connects directly with your website, social media and campaign work, giving the finished content somewhere useful to go.
