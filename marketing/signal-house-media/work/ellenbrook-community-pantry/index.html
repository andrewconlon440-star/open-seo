Selected work · Community

Community support made easier to find and understand.

Ellenbrook Community Pantry provides local food assistance and welcomes community donations. Our work focus is clear, respectful information that helps people understand how to receive support or contribute.

ClientEllenbrook Community Pantry
LocationEllenbrook, Western Australia
FocusAwareness · Information · Community

The opportunity

Help must be easy to find
when people need it.

Community information has to work for people in very different situations. Someone may need food assistance today, want to donate, or simply need the correct opening time and entrance.

The work prioritises dignity, accuracy and plain language. Essential details come first and community context supports them.

The response

Useful information, respectfully shared.

01

Clear access details

Opening times, location and entry information are easy to scan and understand.

02

Donation guidance

03

Community visibility

Consistent local information makes the pantry easier to discover and share.

04

Respectful language

Every message protects dignity while remaining direct and useful.

Existing client strength
