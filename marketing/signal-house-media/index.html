Useful thinking

Advice you can use before you call us.

Good work starts with a clear decision. These short guides explain how we think about three common business problems.

Websites

What a Perth service business website needs

Read guide

A strong service website answers four questions quickly: what do you do, where do you do it, why should someone trust you, and what should they do next? Put the clearest answer near the top of every important page.

Create one useful page for each genuine service and location combination. Support those pages with real photos, specific examples, customer evidence and a direct enquiry path. Remove claims that cannot be checked.

Measure calls and qualified enquiries, then improve the pages people actually use. Traffic alone does not tell you whether the website is helping the business.

Systems

When to buy software and when to build it

Read guide

Buy a proven product when it handles the important workflow, has sensible support and can exchange information with the systems you already use. A good fit saves time immediately.

Consider custom software when staff repeatedly work around the product, important information is trapped between tools, or the process gives your business a real advantage. Map the process before writing any code.

Start with the smallest version that removes a real bottleneck. Measure the time saved and the errors removed before adding more features.

Content

How to plan content that earns attention

Read guide

Begin with the questions customers ask before they choose. Turn those questions into useful stories, demonstrations and explanations instead of filling a calendar with disconnected posts.

Film real work whenever possible. One well planned production day can create a main story, service clips, customer education, still images and short social edits.

Publish with a purpose. Each piece should help someone understand, trust, remember or act. Watch the response and use it to choose the next story.
